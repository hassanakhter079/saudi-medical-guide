DataFlow Verification

Primary Source Verification — Mandatory first step for all Gulf countries

600–900 SAR
3–4 weeks
▾
What is DataFlow? — DataFlow contacts your university, PMC, and hospital directly to confirm your documents are genuine. You cannot book the SMLE or proceed with Mumaris without a positive DataFlow report.

Required Documents

  • Valid Passport (image size must be under 4.5 MB)
  • MBBS Degree Certificate — must be in English or with certified English translation
  • Academic Transcripts — all years, English version
  • PMC Full Registration Certificate (not provisional — only after house job completion)
  • House Job/Internship Completion Certificate (from hospital)
⚠️ Work Experience Verification

DataFlow does NOT officially require experience certificate verification. However, it is strongly recommended you get it verified anyway — some employers specifically ask for a DataFlow-verified experience letter during hiring. Better to have it and not need it than to face a delay after receiving a job offer.

Optional but Recommended

  • House Job / Internship Completion Certificate — DataFlow-verified (recommended for employer requests)

Step-by-Step Process

  • 1Go to dataflowgroup.com → click "Start Verification"
  • 2Select Country: KSA → Organization: Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS)
  • 3Create account using email + phone number (OTP verification required)
  • 4Upload passport + degree certificates — ensure each file is under 4.5 MB
  • 5Select verification package (600–900 SAR range based on number of documents)
  • 6Enter all details carefully — issuing authority, dates, names EXACTLY as on documents
  • 7Complete payment via debit/credit card
  • 8Save your Case Reference Number — required for Mumaris Plus registration
  • 9Wait 3–4 weeks → track progress on DataFlow dashboard
  • 10Download your positive DataFlow report once issued

Mumaris Plus Registration (SCFHS)

Professional Classification — requires DataFlow report first

200 SAR
24–72 hrs for eligibility number
▾
Prerequisite:

You MUST have a completed positive DataFlow report before submitting your Mumaris application. The system requires your DataFlow Case Reference Number during registration.

Documents to Upload

  • MBBS Degree Certificate
  • Academic Transcripts (all years)
  • DataFlow Verification Report (for degree)
  • DataFlow Verification Report (for PMC license)
  • PMC Full Registration Certificate
  • Good Standing Certificate from PMC (valid 6 months — time carefully!)
  • House Job Completion Certificate (Nishtar, signed + stamped)
  • Valid Passport copy

Step-by-Step Registration

  • 1Go to portal.scfhs.org.sa → click "Sign Up" on Mumaris+
  • 2Enter passport details, date of birth, nationality
  • 3Fill personal information — name EXACTLY as per passport, religion, marital status
  • 4Enter address details — country, address, building number, postal code
  • 5Enter permanent professional email + phone number → create password
  • 6Verify account via email link → log in → enter OTP
  • 7Dashboard → click "Get Your Registration"
  • 8Select Profession: Medicine and Surgery | Rank: General Practitioner
  • 9Questionnaire — practice discontinuity, penalties: select "No" for all typically
  • 10Enter Educational Details — university, degree title, graduation date → upload documents
  • 11Enter DataFlow Case Reference Number
  • 12Training/Internship — enter start/end dates, upload Nishtar completion certificate
  • 13Work Experience — if no post-qualification experience, re-enter internship details
  • 14License Details — PMC details, specialization, expiry date → upload PMC certificate + DataFlow report
  • 15Final Review — check all data carefully → upload passport → pay 200 SAR
  • 16Check "Requests" section to track application status
  • 17Receive Eligibility Number by email (within 24–72 hours) — use on Prometric to book exam
💡 Support tip:

If you face issues (e.g., university not listed), use the "Tawasul" feature in the portal side menu. Response within 48–72 hours.

Prometric Exam (SMLE)

Saudi Medical Licensing Exam — bookable from Pakistan (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad)

~280–290 USD
Results in 2–6 weeks
▾

Exam Basics

FormatMultiple Choice Questions (MCQs) — Computer-based
Structure3 sections × 100 questions = 300 MCQs total (up to 20 may be unscored pilot questions)
Duration6 hours total | 120 minutes per section + 2 scheduled breaks (45 min total)
Passing score560 out of 800 (scaled score) — approximately 70%
Cost~$280–290 USD
ResultsReleased within 2–6 weeks after the end of the testing window
AttemptsUp to 4 times per year (3 times per year outside Saudi Arabia)
Centers in PakistanLahore, Islamabad, Karachi
⚠️ Note on Results Timing

Results are NOT instant. After the testing window closes, SCFHS runs psychometric analysis. Expect your results 2–6 weeks after your exam date — plan your timeline accordingly.

Booking Steps

  • 1Go to prometric.com → click "Find My Exam"
  • 2Search: "Saudi Commission for Health Specialties"
  • 3Use "Locate" to find centers in your city
  • 4Select specialty: General Practitioner → search available dates
  • 5Click "Schedule" → enter Eligibility ID + first 4 letters of last name
  • 6Enter personal details, address, passport info accurately
  • 7Pay ~$280–290 USD by card → receive confirmation email

Study Resources

  • First Aid for SMLE — highly recommended for GPs
  • SCFHS Official Blueprint — download from SCFHS website for exact subject breakdown
  • CanadaQBank / ExamCure — for daily MCQ practice (50–100 Qs/day)
  • WhatsApp Groups — join "Pakistani Doctors in Saudi Arabia" for shared materials
  • SCFHS official mock exam (free on portal) — do this in Month 3

Process

QVP — Qualification Verification Program

Final step before visa stamping — Ministry of Human Resources, Saudi Arabia

Visa Stage
2–3 weeks
▾
Purpose: QVP confirms that the job being offered matches your actual qualifications (e.g., a GP is hired for a GP role, not mislabeled). Required for visa stamping.

Key Facts

Managed bySaudi Ministry of Human Resources (PACC)
Which degree?Only your HIGHEST degree (FCPS/Masters if applicable, otherwise MBBS)
University fees?None — QVP is a quality check, not traditional verification
Processing time2–3 weeks
Occupation selectionSelect "Chief Physician" for doctors

Step-by-Step QVP Application

  • 1Go to official QVP website → create account with email and password
  • 2Verify account via OTP sent to email
  • 3Enter profile — First Name, Last Name, Gender, Nationality, Date of Birth
  • 4Enter Passport Number + expiry date
  • 5Set current residence → Reason of Request: "For Job"
  • 6Upload passport copy
  • 7Provide digital signature (type your name)
  • 8Enter university name and full address (include building name and zip code)
  • 9Educational level: Bachelor's for MBBS | Master's for FCPS/MD — Field: Medicine
  • 10Enter enrollment start and end dates exactly as on transcript
  • 11Upload Certificate (Transcript) + Award (Degree)
  • 12Enter exact degree title and award date
  • 13Confirm Job Offer Letter → Occupation: "Chief Physician" → enter hospital name
  • 14Upload Job Offer Letter
  • 15Review all data → check acknowledgement boxes → pay by card
  • 16Wait 2–3 weeks → Saudi MFA notified → visa processing begins

Critical Warnings

Common mistakes that cause delays or rejections

Must Read
▾
⛔ Name Consistency — #1 Cause of Rejection

Your name AND father's name must be IDENTICAL on ALL documents — Passport, CNIC, MBBS degree, PMC certificate, transcripts, Nishtar letter. Even a single letter difference can cause visa rejection. Check all documents NOW before submitting anything.

General Warnings Checklist

  • Verify name spelling is identical across ALL documents before submitting anything
  • Do NOT get Good Standing Certificate from PMC too early — it expires in 6 months
  • Do NOT use provisional PMC license — must be full registration (after house job)
  • Nishtar house job letter must include: job title, start/end dates, role description, signed + stamped
  • Use a permanent professional email for ALL platforms — not a temporary one
  • Do NOT book Prometric until fully prepared (rescheduling is difficult and may incur fees)
  • Save ALL case reference numbers and confirmation emails in a dedicated folder
  • Do DataFlow yourself — agents charge significantly more for the exact same process
  • Bring SAR 5,000–10,000 cash reserves when landing — first Saudi salary arrives 2–3 months later
  • Check your documents from abroad are in English — if in another language, get certified English translation from the university itself
